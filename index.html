    Beyond Civilization

    Five islands. Five tribes, each grown from one man and one woman. You never give orders. You command the sky, the sea and the sickness, and they decide what to do about it.

    • Look around: WASD or the arrow keys move, the mouse wheel zooms, drag to pan, right-drag to orbit. Q/E rotate, T/G tilt, R faces north. Push the mouse to the map edge to scroll, or click the minimap to jump.
    • Choose a power on the left, then click the map to cast it. Energy refills from their faith.
    • Watch them marry, build boats, feud and fall sick. Click villages and named people to read their story.
    • The long test: can they ever reach the Far Shore, out of sight across the deep sea?
